About UrbanBite

UrbanBite is a restaurant management project designed to make browsing a menu, adding dishes to a cart, placing an order and generating a bill feel simple and smooth.

Made by Hitesh Narula Student of Thapar College

Built as a student project with a focus on clean design and a convenient digital dining experience.
